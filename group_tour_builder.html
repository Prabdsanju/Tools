Tour Itinerary

Departure Procedure:

  • Sun Leisure World's representative will meet you at Colombo Bandaranayake International Airport.
  • You will get a briefing of the departure procedure & our representative will guide you in the process.
Day 01 – Pattaya
  • Arrival to Bangkok Don Mueang International Airport.
  • On the way to Pattaya there will be a stop for breakfast. (Not included in the package)

Sightseeing & Tours:

Pattaya Big Buddha Temple Tour with Viewpoint
Experience a panoramic view of the city and marvel at the sheer size of the Big Buddha Statue at Wat Phra Yai.

Pattaya Tiger Park
View and observe different types of tigers including giant Bengal tigers. Optional: Go in the cage to touch and play with tigers.

Pattaya Dolphinarium
Number 01 marine mammal show in Asia, featuring South American fur seals and bottlenose dolphins.

Hotel: Baron Beach Hotel Pattaya 3* (Check-in at 02.00 pm)

Day 02 – Pattaya

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch

Sightseeing & Tours: Coral Island Tour by Speed Boat

  • 09:00 AM transfer to Coral Island by speedboat.
  • Optional: Parasailing, jet skiing, underwater walk en route.
  • Lunch will be served at an Indian restaurant (Complimentary basic lunch).

Overnight Stay at Hotel

Day 03 – Bangkok

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Dinner

  • Check out from Pattaya hotel & transfer to Bangkok.

Sightseeing & Tours:

Sea Life Bangkok Ocean World + Madame Tussauds
Experience fascinating marine life and lifelike celebrity figures.

Chao Phraya Cruise Dinner
05:00 PM pickup for a magical evening river cruise encompassing rich Thai royal history and illuminated landmarks with an international buffet.

Hotel: Picnic Hotel Bangkok 3*

Day 04 – Bangkok

Meal Plan: Breakfast, Lunch

Sightseeing & Tours: Dream World & Snow Town

  • 09:00 AM pickup for a fun-filled day at a popular amusement park.
  • Enjoy adventure activities, thrill rides, and the Snow Town experience.
  • 04:00 PM depart from Dream World.

Overnight Stay at Hotel

Day 05 – Bangkok

Meal Plan: Breakfast

Free time for shopping!
Explore popular locations like Platinum Mall, Indra Market, Big C, Palladium Mall, and Central World. The hotel is within walking distance of shopping complexes.

Overnight Stay at Hotel

Day 06 – Departure

Meal Plan: Breakfast

  • Hotel check out at 12:00 PM (Luggage can be left at lobby).
  • 04:00 PM: Pickup from hotel and transfer to DMK International Airport.
  • Departure to Colombo.
